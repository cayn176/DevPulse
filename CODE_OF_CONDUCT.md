# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate of different experience levels and backgrounds. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and unwanted sexual attention are not acceptable.

## Scope

This applies to project discussions, issues, pull requests, and other spaces where someone represents DevPulse.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation that violates this standard. Contact the project owner privately through the repository's security or contact channel once configured. Until then, use GitHub's private reporting tools where available.

This initial policy is intentionally concise; the project owner should review and adopt a fuller community policy before opening contributions broadly.
